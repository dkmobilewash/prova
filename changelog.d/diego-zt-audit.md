### Zero-touch audited: doors priced at the company's scope, windows at what the product bids, board gaps visible

The audit hand-traced three sets and found the key side pricing doors unconditionally while the product's
default door scope (BY_OTHERS) bids $0 on both sides — the $62k→$104k "doors" cause was that artifact moving
under a rule change, not product error. Doors now price through the scope (headline at the default; FRAMES and
DOORS_AND_FRAMES reported per group), windows price $0 in the headline (no window bid line exists) with the
informational line kept, and key wall feet whose type the heights key does not cover report as UNPRICED board
SF instead of silently zero — the audit found Hutto's whole board term vanishing on a type-spelling mismatch.
