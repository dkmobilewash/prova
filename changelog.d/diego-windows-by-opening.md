### Windows counted by their openings, typed by the nearest tag (Diego)
`diego/windows-by-opening`

NOT FOR MERGE YET: false counts are not low enough (see below).

One tag often names several windows (Topeka: an "A" hexagon read 24 times
against 50–61 windows a sheet), so counting tags undercounted by half. A window
is now the frame drawn in an exterior wall, and the nearest tag of the sheet's
vocabulary within 3 ft of the frame's line gives its type; a frame with no tag
that near is not counted. Where the schedule's marks are single letters, a
letter in the same symbol as a scheduled mark is a window tag too (Topeka's
schedule read has A/D/E/F, its plans tag B and C as well).

And a wall is exterior only against walls of its own drawing: Crosslines A100
prints two plans 21 ft apart on the paper, and no facade was exterior.
