### An architectural sheet that says FLOOR PLAN gets measured, whatever else its title joins

The first whole-set run over every plan set on disk found no crash, no timeout and no sheet too dense. It found buildings going to zero silently: a title like "FLOOR PLAN & DETAILS" was refused on the word "DETAILS", and Fairgrounds, Alden and Crosslines lost their only plan with no error. An A-series sheet that names a floor plan before any other drawing is now measured. Trade, demolition, finish, code and life-safety floor plans stay refused.

Check: on the Fairgrounds set, A-101 FLOOR PLAN & DETAILS must get walls from a whole-set run.
