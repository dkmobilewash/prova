### The Buyout page shows what the spec says before you buy (Diego)
`diego/buyout-spec-findings`

A named product or a rating sat in the bid's spec findings while the buyer
looked at a price on another screen. Each Buyout row now lists the findings
that apply to it, with the quote and page: named products, fire ratings,
acoustic, finish level and performance. Contract terms stay on the bid.

It shows and does not judge, as Diego decided. A job line has no spec section,
so a finding reaches a line by trade, from the section number. Nothing is
dropped: what cannot be placed appears at the top as "not tied to one line".

The page says how many sections were read and that unread sections are not
included. A test forbids any sentence that reads as clearance. There is no model
call and no schema change, and the check imports nothing from `lib/takeoff`.
