### Takeoff: a printed tag also names the other walls it sits beside — when no legend has been read

- A printed tag named only its single nearest run. Totem Lake sets "1" between two parallel partitions and at T-junctions, so the second wall beside it went untagged into the sheet's "rest" question under the sheet's majority type. Diagnosis on the training type keys (touch-free walls, no-legend preset arm): the largest shared cause of feet still needing a press was walls whose own type is tagged on the sheet but not on them (330 ft over 6 sheets); 182 ft of it had their own type's tag as their nearest tag, within reach and beside them mid-span.
- `runsBesideTag`: an untagged run takes its nearest tag when it is within `TAG_REACH_FEET`, falls beside the run mid-span, and every tag of another name is `TAG_CLEAR_MARGIN` times farther; leader-placed tags excluded. On only with no legend read (the mid-span switch).
- Touch-free walls (estimator answers each question right; tags answered as themselves only when they are the set's types): training 69.9% → 72.9% (Totem 40.8 → 69.0, McAllen 73.3 → 78.8, Green Bay A3.2 79.6 → 83.0, no sheet worse); held-out, measured once, 56.2% → 59.6% (Hutto A102 58.3 → 68.7, O'Reillys 50.3 → 55.7, no sheet worse). Tags right: training 31.2 → 35.7%, held-out 23.5 → 27.4%. Legend arm unchanged (61.4%).
