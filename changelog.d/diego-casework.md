### What actually changed, in plain English (Diego)
`diego/casework`

**A thin line pair two feet off a wall is the front of the casework.** Base
cabinets and counters are drawn as a line 24 in off the wall, often with a
second line an inch or two from it, and that pair read as a thin wall. A run
no thicker than 4 in, lying along a parallel candidate for most of its
length with its face 20–28 in from that candidate's face, is set aside as
`casework` and reported like the other set-aside reasons. Swept on every
labelled sheet against main: 501 ft of junk removed for 74 ft of real wall
on training (6.8 to 1, no building worse by more than 34 ft); the held-out
delta is 144 ft of junk for 90 ft of real, with Gen BBQ A101 losing 63 ft
for nothing — recorded in `NOTES.md` for the merge decision.
