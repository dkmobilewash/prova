### What an addendum's sheets change, by wall type

Revised sheets uploaded as a new plan file are matched to the sheets they
replace by sheet number, and a new page, linked from the Takeoff tab, shows
what that does to the takeoff: each wall type's feet before and after, the
change in square feet at its height, and in dollars from its own priced lines,
with the net. A revised run on the same spot as an earlier one keeps its type;
a run with no earlier twin is "new walls, no type yet", priced once typed. Each
sheet says how many of its runs matched in place, so a reissue that moved the
drawing on the page is visible rather than silently untyped.

Apply puts it on the takeoff: each revised wall onto its carried type (or
untyped), the replaced sheet's walls onto "Walls — dropped", labelled with the
drawing that replaced them, and the lines rewritten; Undo is the review queue's
own. Beside it, the same rows in words to send the GC, with Copy.

The revised sheets are uploaded on that page: the Takeoff tab's own uploader
shows only on a job with no drawing yet, so walk v8 found no way to add an
addendum's sheets to a job that already had its set. The Takeoff tab links to
it from the first drawing on ("Upload revised sheets (addendum)").
