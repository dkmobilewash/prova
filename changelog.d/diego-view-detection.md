### View detection reads a drawing's title, not only its scale caption

A sheet is split into its drawings by titles lettered large and ruled, which
a drafter prints under every view whether or not a scale follows; headings
over notes, legends and schedules become text blocks whose regions no
drawing may claim; and a region shared by stacked drawings is cut at the
caption lines rather than their midpoints, because a caption sits below its
drawing. Measured on the app's own candidate list over 18 training sheets,
leave-one-building-out: 622 ft of junk removed against the viewer's current
pipeline and no real wall lost. Not yet wired into `detectForSheet`; the
three-line change is named in NOTES.
