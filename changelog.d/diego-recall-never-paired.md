### A long face serves the pieces of its other face (Diego)
`diego/recall-never-paired`

The pair test refuses a short face whose long partner overhangs it at both
ends, which is right for a column grid line claiming a door leaf and wrong
for a demising wall drawn as one continuous line with the other face in
per-unit pieces: Crosslines' 92 ft of traced wall came back as nothing.
A pass after the second assembly pass awards those pieces when, together,
they cover at least half the long face at one thickness — a grid line never
has that. Pieces under 4 ft and strokes wider than a drafting pen are left
out. On the 26 training sheets with the classifier on it recovers 209 ft of
key wall and adds 53 ft of extra, a fence and a grid dash-dot line; the
bar for shipping was both numbers improving, so it waits. The never-paired
and merged stages themselves are 83% the grid-bubble deletion; the
attribution and the per-sheet table are in `lib/takeoff/NOTES.md`.
