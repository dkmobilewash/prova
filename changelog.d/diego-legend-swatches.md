### A sheet whose legend draws NEW walls filled and EXISTING walls as outlines is read that way round

Schroeder ES A1.01's legend reads "EXISTING WALL TO REMAIN" beside a plain grey outline and "NEW WALL TO BE
PROVIDED" beside a grey-filled band: the opposite of "grey is existing". Two things kept the legend from
counting. The new-wall row was not read at all, because the row pattern required the words to end at "WALL".
And the existing sample's pen (grey 0.50 at 0.48 pt) is not the pen the plan draws its walls with (grey 0.50
at 0.24 pt), so the pen match found nothing.

Now "NEW WALL TO BE PROVIDED / CONSTRUCTED / BUILT / INSTALLED" reads as the new-wall row, and where a legend
draws NEW filled and EXISTING as an outline, a wall is classified by kind: filled is new; an outline at the
existing sample's grey is existing. Pen width and fill shade are not compared there, because swatches and plans
use different ones. Every other legend (an existing poché against new outlines, two outlines a grey or a weight
apart) keeps the exact pen match.

compare, +26% to −10% with it; 257 ft of existing wall set aside, 0 ft of new wall; no other sheet moves.
