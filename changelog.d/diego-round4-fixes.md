### Split area keeps every square foot; Quantities says what fills it; deleting an item can take its measurements

A split now cuts the room's own outline, so its parts add up to the room (on Joplin a split dining area lost 2.5 sf before, none now). Opening Quantities before Find the walls shows what fills it instead of an empty drawer. Deleting an item offers "Also delete its measurements", ticked. Check: split the Joplin dining area and the floor total does not move.
