### Apply says what the estimate actually moved, and the GC's summary carries it

On a revised A101 with two walls added, the addendum page said "Net change to
our price: $0.00, plus the walls still to be priced", and Apply moved the
estimate +$19,539.73 (walk v8): walls with no type yet are priced as the
company's default partition the moment they are on the takeoff, which the
change rows cannot know. Apply now measures the estimate at sale price before
and after, says it ("The estimate moved +$19,539.73"), and the summary it keeps
for the GC states that figure as the net.
