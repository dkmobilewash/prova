### The opening table checks its door key: every printed door tag beside a found door must be in the key (Diego)
`diego/door-line-22b`

Joplin A-101's three confident "false" doors turned out to be real doors whose printed tags (115, 111,
120) the key had missed, and they held the door line down. `zzOpeningFeatureTable.eval.ts` now writes a
key-completeness line per door-keyed sheet: each door mark the product reads within reach of a found door
must have a key door carrying its tag, or one within 6 ft. `loso.py` lists the uncovered marks for a
person to call (a key gap, or a mark that is not a door's). On Round 22b's labels it found 5 probable
gaps on Macon-Bibb A1-1 and 4 detail callouts on UCCS A2.1.2. The rows are byte-identical with and
without the new line. On the same table the door line failed the pre-registered test (1 unmarked post
against a floor of 50), so no door model ships here.
