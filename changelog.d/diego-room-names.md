### Room names read cleanly from the sheet

Room tags are placed by their printed number, so a name running over a wall no longer lands in the wrong room or none, and detail callouts and drafting words stay out of names. Check: Joplin A-101 names room 100 VESTIBULE rather than A1/ A-621 VESTIBULE.

The finish join now counts leaked regions for the finishes their rooms share, ignores tags the schedule does not know, and lets untagged regions take a finish type when all room-sized neighbours agree. Check: glen-hazel A-102 joins 25 rooms and none takes T7.
