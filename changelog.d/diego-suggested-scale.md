### A sheet with no scale can suggest one from its doors, and a person confirms it

About 1,300 sheets across the real sets go unmeasured because they print no scale or dimension the reader can use. At the true scale, door leaves land on whole inches, so the viewer now tries each standard scale on a sheet with no scale and offers the clear winner: "Suggested 1/8" = 1'-0" from 14 doors: confirm?". Nothing is saved until Confirm, which goes through the ordinary calibration action.

Measured on 218 training sheets with the printed scale hidden: 91 right, 1 wrong, 126 refused. Stair treads were tried and dropped at 5 right, 27 wrong, because hatching looks the same.

Check: open a floor plan with no printed scale and doors on it. The line appears above the drawing, no scale is set, and Not this removes it.
