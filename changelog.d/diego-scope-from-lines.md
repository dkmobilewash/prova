### The GC's scope lists only the walls the price is made of

DEMO-BAKER's walk showed the GC's "Scope by wall type" listing S8B and S8C,
whose legend types have no parts and so no estimate lines, and GYPC1, a
ceiling: the proposal named walls that were not in the number. The scope now
reads only wall items with a live estimate line (untyped walls stay, as their
unpriced footage line), and leaves out ceilings and other area items. The
sub's own proposal page says which measured walls are not in the price, with
a link to give the type its parts; Ask's takeoff quantities still count every
measured wall and say whether each is in the price.
