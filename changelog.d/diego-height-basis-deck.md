### "TO METAL DECK ABOVE" runs to the structure, the one gap a training survey found (Diego)
`diego/height-basis-deck`

A survey of every training legend line and height note about where a wall stops (432
lines, 26 sets; Hutto, O'Reilly's and Hillside excluded) found one real gap: "SEAL TIGHT
TO METAL DECK ABOVE" (SLN 3A, 4A, 5A, EA1) had no basis, because a material word sat
before DECK.

A material word (METAL, STEEL, CONCRETE, COMPOSITE, FLOOR) is now allowed before DECK, and
only before DECK. Camp Bow Wow's "PLATE FASTENED TO CONC. SLAB" is a bottom plate, and the
first draft read it as running to the structure. Re-surveyed after the fix: exactly the four
SLN lines change and nothing else.

Not added, because training never says it: "to sheathing above". Sheathing appears only as
cladding ("CLADDING OVER 5/8" SHEATHING"). Also not added: a bare "STRUCTURE ABOVE" diagram
label (SLN EA1, Nall Wealth 3), since every type carrying one already runs to the
structure by its own words, so it would change nothing.

Heights unchanged, because SLN is not in the heights key: training 30.0% correct / 0 wrong /
67.6% no height; held-out, measured once, 1.3% / 5.9% / 91.0%.
