### What actually changed, in plain English (Cyrus)
`feat/bid-guide-home`

C Stream Bid now opens on a home instead of the bid log. Signing in (or landing on `/bid` while signed in) goes to `/bid/home`: your open bids down the left, each with its five-step bar and one coloured line of where it stands, and in the middle one box — "What are we bidding today?". Paste a GC's invite email and press Start: the SAME reader "Log a bid" uses (behind the same Bid research AI switch, one assistant question from the same allowance) proposes the project, GC, trade and dates, shows them to be checked, and only "Log this bid" saves, through the same `createBidInvitation`. Nothing is created on the paste. "Attach drawings" is a link to the existing Start-an-estimate screen, which lands on the job's takeoff for the upload — no second upload pipeline. Under the box, "Needs you" lists every open job with an ask: a tone pill (a word, not just a colour), the job, its one line, and a button carrying the ask's primary action's words, linking to the job.

Two things it deliberately does not do. There is one chip, "What's due this week?" (the bid log's due view); the mock's "Update prices on open bids" is omitted because Bid's price update lives per estimate ("Update prices on this estimate"), so a home chip would be a button that does nothing. And the guides come from `getOpenJobGuides`, which in this branch is a marked placeholder returning nothing — the real engine replaces `lib/bid/guide/index.ts` at merge, so until then the home shows its empty state.

`BID_HOME` moved from `/bid/bids` to `/bid/home`, so `bidAuthDestination` and `bidAuthHref` now land there; "Home" is first in the Bid nav and the wordmark links to it. `BidLanding` was NOT touched and still hard-codes `redirect_url=%2Fbid%2Fbids` on its four sign-in/sign-up links, so those still land on the bid log (a valid Bid page) until that file's four strings are changed to `%2Fbid%2Fhome`; `app/bid/page.test.ts` now asserts they reach a signed-in Bid page rather than comparing them with `bidAuthHref`.

The check: `/bid/home` has a render test over fixtures and over an empty list (every anchor starts `/bid`, the pills and bar segments are the right words and colours, "Needs you" is exactly the jobs with an ask), and a click-through test of the box (paste, Start, fields filled, nothing saved until Log this bid, the confirmed form reaches `createBidInvitation`). `surface.test.ts` pins `home` as an allowed segment and the sign-in landing; `BID_ROUTES` records the page's access decision.
