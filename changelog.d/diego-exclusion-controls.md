### The wall total, the sentence and the way back from an exclusion are on the page

Chrome clicked "Exclude like this" and "Work area" on Baker A100 and saw walls vanish with no total, no sentence and no way back. All of that lived in the Quantities panel, which starts closed on a window under 1440 px. It now sits above the drawing at every width. The sentence was also being wiped by the page refresh its own save caused, so it now clears only on a sheet change.

The top bar's undo now takes back an exclusion or a work area in place, without a reload, and redo restores it. Door and window marks outside a work area are left out. "Clear exclusions" and "Remove work area" appear whenever a sheet has one, including right after a reload.
