### Specs on the drawings, round 3: three new sets keyed by hand, painting dropped, rated partitions and ceiling assemblies found

The hand key now covers six sets: Baker, Bunkie and Gen-BBQ from #1076, plus three of the new sets, all training sets under #1077 (Wendell, Schroeder, cmb). That is 123 Division 09/07 requirements read off their notes, kept with the plans and off the repo. #1077's held-out sets (USU ASPIRE, Riverbend) are not keyed and never shaped a rule.

| On the 123-item key | Found | False hits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main | 69 | 16 |
| Now | 85 | 4 |

**With the key frozen before these fixes** (117 items; six items were added after a fix surfaced them, each matching one already keyed on another set): main finds 69 with 16 false hits, now 79 with 10.

**General causes fixed:**
- **Painting is another trade, even on drywall:** "shall paint", "provide new paint finish", primer, latex, the sheen a surface receives. Painting was most of Schroeder's 7 false hits and was on every new set.
- **Rated partitions, rated walls, rated ceiling assemblies, ceiling tiles and systems, and access panels in ceilings or partitions are this trade's elements.** "Partition" alone stays too broad.
- **A passing mention of a joist, duct or pipe no longer drops** a requirement about deflection track, a ceiling system or acoustic sealant.
- **Other trades' words match their plurals** (ducts, pipes, dampers), and dampers and glazing are added. This removed fire-damper and glazing notes on the unseen sets.
- **Plumbing and electrical systems are another trade**, and a note that only explains the drawings' symbols is not a requirement. This was first in #1079 and moved here so it can merge without the model.
- **Every architectural sheet series is searched**, including Schroeder's AX detail sheets.

**On eight sets never keyed, the two held-out ones included,** the only notes dropped were ones already judged false. The notes added are rated-wall and ceiling requirements, for example "EXTEND ALL FIRE RATED WALLS STRUCTURE TO STRUCTURE" and "ACCESS PANEL FIRE RATINGS MUST MATCH CEILING ASSEMBLY FIRE RATINGS".
