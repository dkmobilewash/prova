### The review-scale fallback picks the biggest view by area, not width

`set-review-scales.mjs` picks a declined multi-scale sheet's fallback view by AREA, not width. By width, Mack Theater p9's wide-short VCT floor-pattern strip (1/4") beat the first-floor NEW WORK OVERVIEW above it (1/8"), and the answer-key sheet would have measured every traced wall at half length. Found applying the scale step the Round-22 keeper ingestion skipped — the "Scale: not set" on every held-out sheet was that skipped `set-review-scales.mjs` step, not a reader failure; the readings and per-view scales were stored all along.
