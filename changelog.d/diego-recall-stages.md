### What actually changed, in plain English (Diego)
`diego/recall-stages`

**Where the missed real wall goes, on the product path.**
`zzMissStages.eval.ts` attributes every missed foot of training key — on the
path the app runs, not the raw finder — to the stage that lost it: set aside
by a named junk rule, found but off the plan boxes, paired then dropped by a
filter, lost to the greedy pass, refused as a hatch series, merged into a
longer run, or never paired with what is drawn along it said. Feet per
stage with the top buildings, recall and junk per sheet, one row per missed
wall for the pair-rule replay.

What it established is in `NOTES.md`: the two largest stages (277 and 265 ft
of 1,321) are each two or three causes deep, and the three fixes they
suggested were measured and rejected — bridging a face's gap (−220 ft real,
+1,400 junk), hatch neighbours made to run alongside (+27, +1,225) and the
thickness cap at 24 in (−94, +9,209, though Supreme Court alone goes 73% to
98%). No finder behaviour changes in this PR.
