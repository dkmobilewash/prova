### A suggested scale can also come from door tags, the grid, or the sheets beside it

A sheet with no scale could only suggest one from five or more door swings. It can now also use door tags matched to the schedule, labelled grid lines shared with a sheet of known scale, and other sheets in its series if its own doors agree. If two sources disagree, nothing is offered. Confirm is still the only thing that saves a scale.

Measured on 218 training sheets with the printed scale hidden: 125 right, 1 wrong, 92 refused, up from 91 right. Offers on 318 no-scale sheets rose from 36 to 42, because 170 of those sheets have no door swing at all.

Check: open an unscaled plan that shares grid lines with a scaled sheet. The line names the grid as its reason, and no scale is set until Confirm.
