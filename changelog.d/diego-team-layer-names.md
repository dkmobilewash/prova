### A renamed takeoff layer is the team's name for it, saved with the job and in the export

- Since #959, double-clicking a layer row in the full-screen workspace renamed it in that person's browser only. Now the name is saved with the job in a new table, `TakeoffLayerName` (job, layer key, name, who and when).
  - Everybody on the team sees it, on the layer row and on the group's Quantities detail.
  - The Excel export gains a **Wall groups** tab: sheet, group (the team's name, or the default the panel shows), runs, lin ft, and whether the team named it.
  - An empty name clears it, and so does typing the default back.
  - A teammate's rename also trips the other open tab's "changed elsewhere" banner.
- **The key is the group, not a row.** Groups are recomputed from the stored proposals on every load, so a name is keyed on what makes them the same group across a re-find: thickness to the eighth and the drawing's tag (`wall:4.875:A1`), plus `rooms` and `doors`. `lib/takeoff/layerKey.ts` builds it for the workspace, the action and the export alike, and the action refuses a key it could not have built.
- **Its own tab, not the Quantities one.** A group's feet already reach Quantities through whichever item it was added to; the same figure in two rows of one table would read as double.
- **Migration `20261104230000_takeoff_layer_names`**, announced in #prova-build before the push.
  - Additive: `CREATE TABLE`, a unique index on `(jobId, groupKey)` and a foreign key to `Job` with `ON DELETE CASCADE`, so it adds nothing to the cleanup scripts' lists.
  - Generated by a schema-to-schema `prisma migrate diff`.
  - Rehearsed on a populated copy of `prova_dev` (56 jobs, 354 proposals): applied as the 211th migration with no row counts moved; a name saved on a real job, a duplicate refused, and a job delete took its names with it.
