### Doors with no swing are counted from the schedule; a window needs a symbol

- `doorsAndWindowsForSheet` counts a scheduled door the drawing has no
  swing for: the headline is `doorCount()` — drawn doors plus those the
  join placed from a tag — and the panel lists the placed ones and the
  schedule rows no door on the sheet carries. SW Grand Junction A110 reads
  5 of 5 where it read 3: the 10 ft sliding storefront door is placed
  between the two pier runs its tag sits past the ends of
  (`joinOpeningsToSchedule`), and the overhead door from its tag on the
  wall. A mark printed loose, or in a pill the symbol pass does not draw,
  is a mark when the schedule knows it (Bunkie's E101, E102 named).
- `windowSymbols`: without a tag a candidate needs a line strictly inside
  the matched wall's thickness and none an inch past its faces — the
  exterior wall's faces plus a parallel roof or sill line are not a window.
  A tag names its nearest candidate only, and the lites drawn like it.
  Joplin A-101 54 → 44 windows (the 13–15 ft pier-to-pier bands gone, the
  4 ft G windows read one by one); false windows on the keyed sheets:
  Baker 26 → 13, MHK 8 → 3, Crosslines 2 → 0, Bunkie 4 → 4.
