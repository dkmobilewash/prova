### The queue counts a sheet's groups the way its buttons do; the workspace stays editable at 600 px

Chrome on Baker A101 stepped through the queue and counted 10 groups with a type
against "Accept all … (9 groups)", and 10 named by nothing against "on all 11
groups". The data was 9 and 11; one group at a time is easy to miscount. The
queue now says it once beside the buttons: "A101: 20 groups · 9 named by the
drawing · 11 named by nothing", with any suggested by thickness alone counted
apart (Accept all leaves those, since the thickness is a guess).

At 622 px (a laptop window with a side panel open) the workspace opened
read-only with no word why. It is editable down to 600 px now, and below that
the badge says "read-only below 600 px: widen the window to edit".
