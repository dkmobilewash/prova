### What actually changed, in plain English (Diego)
`diego/symbol-count-set`

"Count like this" now counts across the set and knows what a symbol is.
After boxing one symbol, "Find on all sheets" walks every sheet of the same
discipline (or all of them) in the browser, a row and its seconds per sheet
as it goes, trying a sheet's own printed scale where it differs, and saves
the lot as one count item with a per-sheet breakdown — giving a sheet the
scale it prints where it has none and saying which sheets print none. Baker,
65 pages: the electrical sheets in 2 seconds, every sheet in 15.

The matcher learned, against hand keys on Baker's lighting, power and
mechanical plans and Topeka's plumbing: a covering stroke must be the same
pen (an empty ceiling tile no longer counts as a fixture); grey under a
black symbol is background; a circle drawn in two hundred micro-pieces can
be found at all and counts as one feature; the letters inside a symbol and
the type tag beside it are part of it (OS is not PC, E2 is not R2) while a
number inside is the instance; a line running through the box belongs to
it. Troffers 21 of 23 and 0 false, sensors 16 of 16, exit signs 5 of 5, E2
7 of 7, diffusers 17 of 17. What it still cannot do is written down: a
scanned sheet (the raster stage keeps only wall-length lines), text drawn
as paths, and a grey fixture under black pipe.
