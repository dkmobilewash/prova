### A building's long wall is kept whatever is written beside it

The site-equipment rule sets a run aside when a word like PLAY YARD or GATE sits within 10 ft of it. On Camp Bow Wow A102 that removed two exterior walls, 248 ft of real wall, because a label sat near one end. A run over 100 ft is now always kept.

Training: 247 ft of real wall back for 43 ft of junk, 96.2% to 96.9% on new builds. Held-out totals did not move: no real wall gained, no junk added.
