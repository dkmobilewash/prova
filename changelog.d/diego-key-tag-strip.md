### Labelling: the tag strip says which mark it tags, and ⌘Z takes back a tag

Placing a mark focuses it, so a tag picked "for the next mark" landed on the last one, and one ⌘Z couldn't reverse a tag change. Chrome mis-tagged two openings in Round 39 and reloaded without saving.
- The strip now names its target: "→ window 2 (the larger ring), tagged W1", or "no tag yet".
- The review records what was done in order: traces drawn and tags given, each tag with the one it replaced. ⌘Z takes back the newest of them.
- A reopened sheet starts with nothing to undo; what came off disk is the baseline.

Labelling tool only; the customer product never mounts the key panel.
