### A window frame drawn wider than the wall the finder paired is still a window

A window frame had to sit inside the faces of the wall run it was matched to. On Crosslines
and Topeka the frame is drawn 6 to 14 in across, as five or six lines, while the run the
finder paired is a 4 to 6 in pair of faces. So a frame line sat outside the "wall", and 35
real windows on the training sheets were dropped. The rule existed to keep out desk edges
and sill lines beside a wall, which are narrow and drawn in three lines. A frame 2.5 to 5 ft
wide with five or more lines may now reach past the faces; anything else still may not. The
5 ft cap keeps out Joplin's 6 ft pieces of cavity wall between windows, which are drawn just
like a frame.

Training (8 sheets, 279 window marks): 153 to 169 windows suggested, with false unchanged
at 4. Held-out, measured once: no change.

A second rule, suggesting windows from their frames on sheets that print no window tag, was
also measured. It found 59 more training windows with no false ones, but on held-out it
added 25 false windows (Riverbend, USU) and found 1 of USU's 35. It is not in this change.
