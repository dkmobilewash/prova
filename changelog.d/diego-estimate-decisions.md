### Every wall on the estimate, one ceiling source, openings off board

Untyped walls reach the estimate, priced with the default partition and marked "assumed type: check", or as footage with no price. Where a finish schedule covers a room, its ceiling line replaces the ceiling item. Doors and windows come off wall board on Send to estimate. The whole-set run uses split rooms and writes the finish takeoff. Check: on the Wall types page make a default partition, press Send to estimate, and expect a line marked assumed.
