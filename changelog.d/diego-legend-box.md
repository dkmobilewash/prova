### What actually changed, in plain English (Diego)
`diego/legend-box`

**A framed legend, schedule or notes block is set aside whole, not offered
as four walls.** Joplin's CODE FOOTPRINT LEGEND, CODE SUMMARY, GENERAL PLAN
NOTES and FLOOR PLAN LEGEND are each drawn in a double-ruled frame, and the
product offered every side of every frame as a five-inch wall, with the
legend's wall samples inside them. A frame — two uprights whose ends agree,
closed by a rail — is now set aside with everything inside it when a
heading word (LEGEND, SCHEDULE, KEYNOTES, NOTES, SUMMARY, SYMBOLS,
ABBREVIATIONS, WALL TYPES) sits in its top band in large type. Not text
density: that read a row of labelled kennel runs as a schedule and cost 178
ft of real wall in the first sweep, so the rule is title-only. Measured over
all 52 labelled sheets: 697 ft of junk off Joplin's two sheets, no real wall
lost anywhere, every other sheet unchanged, held-out delta zero. Details in
`NOTES.md`.
