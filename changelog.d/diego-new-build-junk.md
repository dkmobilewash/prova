### A stack of dimensions is not a wall

Two dimension strings a wall-thickness apart were being offered as a wall, the largest named junk on the new-build sheets. A pair of lines with four or more dimensions between them and at most three runs meeting it is now set aside, with its reason. An earlier version counted two dimensions, and its held-out result failed the shipping rule, because a real wall often has one or two written across it.

Training: 523 ft of junk gone for 13 ft of real wall, no building worse. Held-out totals: 97 ft of junk gone, no real wall lost.

Check: on Fairgrounds A-101 stacked dimension strings are no longer offered, and the set-aside list names them.
