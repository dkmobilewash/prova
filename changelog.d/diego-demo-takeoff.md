### Demolition takeoff: walls and doors to remove, posted as their own lines

A demolition sheet now has Find demolition. It reads double dashed walls and keynoted doors, lets the estimator untick any run, and saves them as Demo walls and Demo doors. They post through a new Demolition recipe as labor lines in lf and ea, by wall type where tagged.

The check for the trap: a typical floor is read once, so the quantity is not multiplied by the floors it stands for, and a "TYP." door keynote undercounts. The panel says so for doors. Multiply a typical floor by hand.
