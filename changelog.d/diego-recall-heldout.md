### What actually changed, in plain English (Diego)
`diego/recall-heldout`

**Where held-out recall goes, measured as totals, and the one rule that
followed.** The stage instrument gained a held-out mode that prints feet per
stage, per building and by view count and nothing finer; it says 45% of the
held-out miss is the in-a-hatch set-aside, seven times training's rate. Two
probes then asked what that class holds: on training labels every bucket is
junk, and on held-out, as totals, the hidden wall is 16–30 in thick with
hatch between two faces — masonry above the pairer's cap — which on
training is existing construction outside the key. A banded-fill rule for
3–16 in bands is included and measured: +43 ft real for +55 ft of extra on
training, the extra being unlabelled rated walls, and 0 / 0 on held-out.
Nothing here changes a held-out number; the finding is that the two keys
disagree about a hatched masonry band. Details in `NOTES.md`.
