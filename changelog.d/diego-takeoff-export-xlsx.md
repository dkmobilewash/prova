### Download the takeoff as Excel or CSV

Two buttons beside Send to estimate download the job's takeoff, built in the browser with nothing stored. Quantities has one row per item, sheet and source (found, traced, count, assumed, to confirm): walls by type, rooms one by one, ceilings, doors and windows by mark, counts, demolition and finish lines. Sheets lists every sheet, counted or skipped and why. Check: on a job with a whole-set run, Download Excel opens with both tabs.
