### The printed scale is on the toolbar, and a scanned Find is lighter

A sheet with no scale that reads a printed one now shows it on the toolbar. Pressing it opens Set scale with the line drawn on the sheet, and Save is still the estimator's; a reduced print stops at its caution. Find on a scanned sheet no longer opens six other sheets for a template they cannot give. Check: on a sheet with no scale, the button names the scale.
