### A numbered symbol series that runs past a numeric legend is a keynote list, and never types a wall

ROUND 15 noted that Modbox names its walls by detail callouts (1, 2, 4, 5) and numbers its keynotes in ovals. Training has the same case: E Street (legend 1–4) numbers its keynote squares 1 to 17 and MHK (legend 1–4) its circles 1 to 7, each the plan's commonest numbered shape, so keynotes 1–4 typed walls — 175 ft on E Street and 109 ft on MHK, none of it on a wall the key types (MHK's key walls are all unmarked). Where every legend code is a bare number, a symbol shape holding two or more numbers the legend does not define is now a keynote series, and none of its symbols tags a wall (one stray number is a type the legend read missed, not a series). Training: MHK 109 → 0 ft, E Street 191 → 88 ft (from a shape that stays inside the legend), Murray unchanged; the 12 type-keyed training sheets byte-identical (92.5% correct-of-typed). Held-out, measured once: Hutto A101/A103 and O'Reillys unchanged (49.6%); Modbox A1.0 numbered-symbol typing 78 → 7 ft (measure only — its key has no types yet). The wall-type precision causes were also sorted (training, wrong + near 445 ft): tags printed on walls the key types differently (K-4, Joplin A3/A, Green Bay IW-2/IW-4) are drawing-vs-key disagreements sent for a key check, not built against. Check: `symbolTagging.test.ts`, red with the rule removed or its threshold lowered to one.
