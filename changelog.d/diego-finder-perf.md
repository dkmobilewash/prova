### The wall finder is ~40% faster on big sheets, with identical results

ELEVEN03 (342 MB) stalled the server's event loop — 86 s lost in 463 s, Run takeoff to posted 102 s, Accept all over 60 s. Profiled on A4.B.2 (82,825 strokes): the finder 5.2 s, the set-asides 1.4 s, the grouping 0.5 s, and no single stage dominant. What was wasteful: `inAHatchSeries` and `drawnCoverage` recomputed every stroke's angle and length for every candidate (`angleOf`/`lengthOf`, 1.6 s of self time); `bothFacesDashedInPoints` converted the whole sheet to page-width units again for every run (0.64 s plus garbage); `hatchedRuns` ran its rotated test on every stroke for every run. Now each stroke array's geometry and unit conversion are computed once (`segmentGeometry`, same formulas), and `hatchedRuns` refuses a stroke outside the band's bounding box (a hair wider) before the exact test. ELEVEN03 A4.B.1/2/3: 4.6 / 7.2 / 6.2 s → 3.1 / 3.9 / 3.5 s. Identical by proof, not by argument: offered runs, set-asides and groups fingerprinted on six sheets (ELEVEN03 ×3, Baker A101, Crosslines A100, Green Bay A3.1) match, and every offered run on all 37 training sheets (4,344) is byte-identical. Event-loop yields between the phases belong to the queue's own file (`detectWallsForCalibration.ts`, the third's), so they are not in this change. Check: `segmentGeometry.test.ts`; `zzFinderProfile.eval.ts` reproduces the times and fingerprints.
