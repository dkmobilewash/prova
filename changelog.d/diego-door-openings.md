### Takeoff: the door schedule's openings reach the estimate (behind the plan-text read, OFF)

With the plan-text read on, a found-wall group already showed the doors
the schedule placed on its runs. Now each shows width × height ("100A ·
6'-0" × 7'-0" from the schedule"), and adding the group posts those doors
as the wall run's openings, so the board deducts them — or not — by the
company's own rule under Settings (none, large only above a threshold, or
all). A door the schedule prints no height for is named on the group and
left out rather than guessed at seven feet. Under the groups, the
schedule rows no run on this sheet carries are listed as "scheduled, not
found on this sheet", with the count and the schedule page.

The check: plan-text read on, a set with a door schedule, Find the walls
on a floor plan: groups read "door 101 · 3'-0" × 7'-0" from the schedule";
add one priced; on the estimate the board line is smaller than the same
group added with the rule set to "none" — by width × height per door.
