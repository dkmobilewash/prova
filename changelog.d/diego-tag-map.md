### Takeoff: the sheet's own printed wall codes become tags, and one question types the rest of a sheet

- **Printed codes join the tag vocabulary when no legend has been read.** Totem Lake Dental prints 1, 1A, 2A, 2B and 3B beside its walls; with plan reading off the vocabulary was the company's preset (W1–W3), none of those was in it, and the preset's short form read the sheet's "1" as W1. `printedTagCodes` adds short designations printed at least twice (not room/door numbers, not sheet references). With a legend read the legend still decides: adding printed codes beside one cost 61.4% → 46.9% settled-correct on the training type keys, so they stay out there. Training, no legend: tags right on 4.2% → 30.4% of key feet. Held-out, measured once: legend arm unchanged (26.4%); no legend, tags right 2.1% → 16.9%, wrong share of tagged feet 36% → 49%. Those tags name no company type, so nothing posts from them; they only group walls for the estimator.
- **The tag tie-break (prefer the run a tag falls beside mid-span) was measured and NOT shipped**: training with a legend went 61.4% → 60.0% settled-correct.
- **One question per sheet in the review queue**: "The rest of A2.1's untagged walls: N groups, F ft = which of your types?". Nothing preselected; one save, one Undo; "One by one" leaves the rows.
