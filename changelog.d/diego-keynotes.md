### Takeoff: keynotes are no longer read as wall tags (no legend read)

- With no legend, a sheet's printed codes are the tag vocabulary, and keynotes are printed codes: Bunkie's 1–15 (a numbered list, and hexagons on the plan), Green Bay A3.1's 1–18 (diamonds and circles), Joplin's 1–9 (squares). Each became a "Tag 5 = which of your types?" nobody could answer.
- `keynoteItems` leaves out a numbered list's entries and its numbers' copies on the plan when they sit in one symbol shape that holds at least four different listed numbers and isn't the shape the sheet's lettered wall tags use. A code still printed twice elsewhere stays. Totem Lake's 1–4 are listed too, but its pentagons hold three of them, so they stay.
- Training (no legend): bulk questions 78 → 59 (junk-tag questions 41 → 22), presses 115 → 96, touch-free walls 72.9% → 75.3% (Bunkie 53.7 → 100%; no sheet worse). Held-out, once: questions 85 → 80, presses 131 → 125, touch-free 59.6% unchanged. Legend arm unchanged.
