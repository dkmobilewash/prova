### Takeoff: the sheet list names its sheets, ranks the floor plans to scale, and sets every printed scale in one press (behind the plan-text read, OFF)

A 65-sheet set opened as "0 of 65 started · 65 need a scale", with
"Sheet 5… Sheet 10" in the picker and a blank name box on the scale form
until the paid title-block step ran. Now, for a company that has turned
plan-text reading on, the page inventory — which already reads every
page's text with no model — proposes each sheet's number from the title
block's corner and its title when the title's lettering is distinctly
taller than the rest of the block (HIGH from the corner, MEDIUM from the
wider region; a title at the block's own size is not believed). The rail,
the picker and the scale form's name box read them. The rail lists the
floor plans still needing a scale first in their group and says so
("5 floor plans to scale, listed first"), each sheet's chip names the
scale it prints ("1/8" = 1'-0" printed"), and one button per printed
scale sets every unscaled sheet that prints it — each sheet from ITS OWN
reading, the line the inventory anchored and the feet it declared; never
a typed figure, never one sheet's scale copied onto another. A sheet with
no reading is skipped and named.

The check: plan-text read on (Settings → Assistant), upload a set, open
the takeoff workspace: the rail's first rows are the floor plans with
"… printed" chips, the progress line names how many; press "Use 1/8" =
1'-0" on N sheets that print it"; the status line reads "Scale set on N
sheets", the chips turn to "scale set", and opening one of them shows
"Scale set" with a note saying it came from the printed scale. With the
read off, the rail is page order and the chips read "confirm", as before.
