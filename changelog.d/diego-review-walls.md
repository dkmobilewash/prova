### Review walls: one run at a time, K to keep, D to drop, U to undo

After Find the walls, "Review walls" steps through the runs one at a time, zoomed to each, with a counter like "12 of 48 reviewed". K keeps, D drops and U undoes. On a sheet whose walls are already on the job, a dropped run moves to "Walls — dropped", off the estimate, and a kept "to confirm" run goes onto it. Undo puts it back.

Runs come in order of likely junk feet, length times the model's doubt, with the held runs last. With the labels playing the reviewer on the training sheets, a new build reaches +10% of the key after a median of 5 runs, against 26 in plain doubt order.

Check: on Joplin A-101 after a whole-set run, Find the walls, Review walls, press D on the first run. The counter moves, the walls total drops, and U brings it back.
